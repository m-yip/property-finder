The problem

Six agents. A dozen calls. One notes app you stopped updating.

Searching for a home means a dozen agents from different agencies, all calling, emailing and texting. Viewings get booked in three different apps. Callbacks slip. And after five viewings in a weekend, nobody can remember which flat had the damp and which had the light.

It’s the biggest decision most of us make, and it runs on memory and missed calls.
