Privacy, in plain English.

Last updated 17 November 2026

Homeswoon helps you keep track of homes, agents and viewings. To do that we store a small amount of information about you. Here’s what it is, why we need it, and how to have it removed.

What we collect

  • Your email address, and your first name if you give it. This is how we recognise you and keep your boards safe.
  • What you add to your boards: listing links, prices, agents, viewing dates, scores and notes. Anyone you share your board code with can see these too.
  • Whether you want updates, if you tick the box or use an updates form.
  • Messages you send us through the contact form: your email, your name if you give it, and what you wrote.
  • How the site is used: simple events such as “added a home” or “joined a board”, tied to a random ID stored in your browser, plus which link or campaign brought you here.

We don’t collect payment details during the beta, and we don’t use advertising trackers.

Why we use it

  • To run the service you signed up for: saving your boards and letting the people you invite see them.
  • To send you updates about new features and the beta, only if you asked for them. Every email has a way to stop them, and you can withdraw consent any time.
  • To improve Homeswoon: understanding which features people use, in aggregate.

We never sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

Who helps us run it

We use a few trusted services to host the site and store data:

  • Railway hosts the app and its database.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website.
  • Messages from the contact form are saved in our database on Railway, delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, and that inbox is hosted by Google (Gmail). Google also serves the site’s fonts.
  • When you paste a listing link, our server (and occasionally a public page-fetching service) reads that public listing page to fill in its details.

Some of these providers may store data outside the UK. Where they do, they use the standard legal safeguards for international transfers.

How long we keep it

Your account and boards are kept while you use Homeswoon. If you ask us to delete them, we’ll do so within 30 days. Your place on the updates list is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or stop updates at any time. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can also contact the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Questions, or want your data removed?
Send us a message or email hello.homeswoon@gmail.com, and we’ll reply within a few days.
