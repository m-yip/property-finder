Questions, answered.

Homeswoon is in beta, so it’s free and still growing. Here are the things people ask most. If your question isn’t here, send us a message at the bottom of the page.

What is Homeswoon?

A calm place to keep your house hunt together. Paste a listing link and Homeswoon pulls in the photos, price and agent. Then track viewings, score each home and add notes, all on one board instead of spread across tabs, emails and screenshots.

Is it free?

Yes, everything is free while Homeswoon is in beta. If that ever changes, we’ll tell you well before it does.

What does “beta” mean here?

The app works and your boards are saved, but we’re still adding features and smoothing rough edges. You might spot the odd glitch. If you do, please tell us using the form below. It really helps.

Which property sites does it work with?

Paste a link from Rightmove, Zoopla, OnTheMarket, OpenRent or most estate agents’ own websites. If we can’t read a page, choose Enter details manually and add the home yourself.

What happens when a listing is taken down?

Agents often remove adverts once a home is under offer or let. When that happens the home is marked No longer listed, and everything you saved stays on your board: photos, price, notes and scores.

Can I search with my partner or housemates?

Yes. Every board has a code. Share it, and anyone who enters it with I have a board code sees the same board, so you can compare scores and notes together. Only share it with people you trust, as they can see and edit everything on the board.

Can estate agents see my notes?

No. Agents never see your board. Only people you’ve given your board code to can see it.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Homeswoon runs in your phone’s browser, so there’s nothing to install. It’s handy for scoring a home while you’re still at the viewing.

How do I delete my data?

Send us a message below choosing Data or privacy request, and we’ll delete your account and boards within 30 days. Our privacy notice explains what we store and why.

How do I stop update emails?

Every update email has a link to stop them. You can also ask us using the form below.

Still have a question?

Send us a message and we’ll get back to you by email, usually within a couple of days.
