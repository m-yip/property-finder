A dozen calls. Six different estate agents. Notes you can’t keep up with. It’s a lot.

It’s the biggest decision most of us make, squeezed around work and life, with no time for more admin.

How Homeswoon helps

One calm place for the whole search.

From the first listing you save to the flat you can’t stop thinking about, every step lives on one shared board.

01 — Add a home

Every estate agent, every listing, one board.

Paste a link from any portal or agency site and Homeswoon fills in the price, size, photos and agent for you. No more tabs, screenshots or spreadsheets.
